# Chinook Product & Music Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses the product and music sales data within the Chinook database using SQL.

The analysis focuses on artist performance, album revenue, track-level contributions, monthly revenue trends, and genre performance.

The goal was to use SQL to explore sales patterns and identify which artists, albums, tracks, and genres performed best.

## Tools Used

- MySQL
- SQL
- Chinook Database

## Business Questions

The analysis answers the following questions:

1. Which are the top 5 artists by total sales revenue?

2. What is the monthly revenue generated by each artist, and what is their running total over time?

3. For each album, which track generated the most revenue, and what percentage of the album's total revenue did it contribute?

4. Which artists experienced a decrease in monthly revenue compared with the previous month?

5. Which artist generated the highest sales for each genre?

## Key Analysis Areas

### Artist Performance

Artists were ranked based on their total sales revenue to identify the top-performing artists in the database.

### Monthly Artist Revenue

Monthly revenue was calculated for each artist, along with a running total to examine how revenue accumulated over time.

### Album & Track Performance

Track-level revenue was analysed within each album to identify the highest-revenue track or tracks and determine their contribution to total album revenue.

Where multiple tracks generated the same highest revenue, all tied tracks were retained in the results.

### Revenue Declines

Monthly revenue was compared with the previous month's revenue using the `LAG()` window function to identify artists whose revenue decreased from one month to the next.

### Genre Performance

Artists were ranked within each genre based on sales revenue to identify the top-selling artist for each genre.
